- **Animism** (Prehistoric foundational belief, present in many indigenous traditions): "All things in nature possess spirits or souls that must be respected."
  ⚠️ "Viewed as primitive superstition lacking empirical evidence."
  🎯 "Harmonious coexistence between humans, nature, and spirits for communal balance."
  ⚔️ "Benevolent spirits vs malevolent ones; shamans mediate through rituals to restore balance."

- **Ancient Polytheism** (Foundational in early civilizations like Mesopotamian, Egyptian, Greek, Roman, Norse): "Multiple gods embody natural forces, human traits, and cosmic order, honored through rituals."
  ⚠️ "Promotes moral relativism or superstition by anthropomorphizing deities."
  🎯 "Society maintained in cosmic balance through offerings, festivals, and divine favor."
  ⚔️ "Gods of order (e.g., Zeus, Ra) vs chaos forces/titans; battles fought via myths, heroes, and human piety."

- **Zoroastrianism** (Ancient Iranian, ~1500–1000 BCE, influenced Abrahamic dualism): "Good thoughts, good words, good deeds in service to truth and righteousness."
  ⚠️ "Rigid dualism seen as oversimplifying morality or justifying conflict."
  🎯 "Purified world where truth prevails, leading to final renovation (Frashokereti)."
  ⚔️ "Ahura Mazda (wise lord) and Asha (truth) vs Angra Mainyu (destructive spirit); humans choose sides through free will, final cosmic battle."

- **Hinduism** (Ancient Indian/Vedic origin, oldest continuing tradition): "Eternal dharma sustains the universe; achieve moksha (liberation) from rebirth cycle."
  ⚠️ "Caste system historically entrenched social inequality and discrimination."
  🎯 "Society ordered by dharma and varna roles, progressing toward universal spiritual liberation."
  ⚔️ "Devas and Trimurti (Brahma/Vishnu/Shiva) vs asuras; cyclical battles preserved by divine avatars."

  -- **Advaita Vedanta** (Non-dualistic school): "Brahman is the sole reality; self (Atman) is identical to it, world is illusion (maya)."
    ⚠️ "Denies reality of the world, leading to detachment from social issues."
    🎯 "Enlightened society realizing oneness beyond all divisions."
    ⚔️ "No ultimate evil; ignorance (avidya) overcome solely by knowledge, no personified battle."

  -- **Bhakti Traditions** (Devotional paths, e.g., Vaishnavism/Shaivism): "Loving devotion to a personal deity grants grace and liberation."
    ⚠️ "Emotional excess or superstition over rational inquiry."
    🎯 "Devotional communities united in divine love."
    ⚔️ "Devotion aligns with divine will against ego and materialism."

- **Jainism** (Ancient Indian, emphasis on non-violence): "Non-violence (ahimsa) and asceticism liberate the soul from karma."
  ⚠️ "Extreme asceticism impractical or harmful to human flourishing."
  🎯 "Peaceful society free of harm, souls achieving pure liberation."
  ⚔️ "No creator god; internal battle against karmic particles through self-discipline, no external evil leader."

- **Buddhism** (Indian origin, ~5th century BCE): "End suffering (dukkha) through the Noble Eightfold Path to enlightenment (Nirvana)."
  ⚠️ "Views life as inherently suffering, fostering passivity or withdrawal."
  🎯 "Compassionate society of awakened beings free from greed, hatred, delusion."
  ⚔️ "No supreme god/evil; internal struggle against Mara (temptation) and three poisons, won through mindfulness."

  -- **Theravada** (Southern/Elder school): "Individual arahat liberation via discipline and original teachings."
    ⚠️ "Seen as overly monastic and less accessible to laypeople."
    🎯 "Society supporting monastic path to personal Nirvana."
    ⚔️ "Emphasis on personal effort against defilements."

  -- **Mahayana** (Northern/Great Vehicle): "Bodhisattva vow to enlighten all beings before oneself."
    ⚠️ "Complex cosmology risks superstition."
    🎯 "Universal enlightenment through compassion."
    ⚔️ "Bodhisattvas aid against suffering's causes."

  -- **Vajrayana** (Tantric/Diamond Vehicle): "Rapid enlightenment via esoteric practices and guru guidance."
    ⚠️ "Secret rituals open to misuse or exploitation."
    🎯 "Transformed society through awakened energy."
    ⚔️ "Transmutes negative forces into wisdom."

- **Confucianism** (Chinese, ~5th century BCE, philosophy/doctrine): "Cultivate ren (benevolence) and li (ritual propriety) for harmonious order."
  ⚠️ "Rigid hierarchy suppresses individuality and innovation."
  🎯 "Stable, ethical society through education, family, and just governance."
  ⚔️ "No supernatural battle; moral self-cultivation vs chaos/disorder through example and rites."

- **Taoism/Daoism** (Chinese, ancient): "Live in harmony with the Tao (the natural way), embracing simplicity and flow."
  ⚠️ "Promotes passivity or avoidance of responsibility."
  🎯 "Spontaneous, balanced society without forced control."
  ⚔️ "Yin-yang balance; no absolute evil, opposition harmonized through wu wei (non-action)."

- **Shinto** (Japanese indigenous): "Honor kami (spirits) through purity, rituals, and harmony with nature."
  ⚠️ "Lacks deep ethical code or universal truth claims."
  🎯 "Pure, harmonious community connected to ancestors and land."
  ⚔️ "Kami of order vs polluting forces; purified through rituals, no ultimate evil leader."

- **Judaism** (Ancient Abrahamic, ~2000 BCE): "One God in covenant with His people; follow Torah for ethical monotheism."
  ⚠️ "Exclusivity or 'chosen people' concept fosters insularity."
  🎯 "Just world repaired (tikkun olam), awaiting messianic peace."
  ⚔️ "God and righteous vs yetzer hara (evil inclination); battled through mitzvot (commandments) and repentance."

- **Christianity** (Abrahamic, 1st century CE): "Salvation from sin through faith in Jesus Christ as God incarnate."
  ⚠️ "Historical violence (crusades, inquisitions) and doctrinal divisions."
  🎯 "Kingdom of God realized in love, justice, and eternal life."
  ⚔️ "God/Trinity and angels vs Satan/demons; spiritual warfare, culminating in Christ's return."

  -- **Roman Catholicism**: "Salvation through faith, sacraments, and Church authority."
    ⚠️ "Papal infallibility and clerical scandals undermine credibility."
    🎯 "Universal Church guiding humanity to God."
    ⚔️ "Saints and Mary intercede in the battle."

  -- **Eastern Orthodoxy**: "Mystical union with God (theosis) via liturgy and tradition."
    ⚠️ "Resistance to change or ecumenism."
    🎯 "Communion of divine-human harmony."
    ⚔️ "Emphasis on icons and hesychasm in spiritual struggle."

  -- **Protestantism**: "Justification by faith alone, scripture as sole authority."
    ⚠️ "Fragmentation into thousands of denominations."
    🎯 "Personal relationship with God in reformed society."
    ⚔️ "Direct access to God strengthens individual warfare."

- **Islam** (Abrahamic, 7th century CE): "Complete submission to Allah; Muhammad as final prophet."
  ⚠️ "Interpretations permitting violence or restricting freedoms."
  🎯 "Global ummah under divine law (Sharia), paradise for faithful."
  ⚔️ "Allah and angels vs Iblis/shayatin; greater/lesser jihad, final Day of Judgment."

  -- **Sunni**: "Leadership via consensus and sunnah of companions."
    ⚠️ "Historical caliphate conflicts."
    🎯 "Community-guided orthodoxy."

  -- **Shia**: "Imamate from Ali's lineage as divinely appointed."
    ⚠️ "Martyrdom culture or sectarian tension."
    🎯 "Just rule awaiting hidden Imam."
    ⚔️ "Emphasis on suffering and intercession."

- **Sikhism** (Indian, 15th century CE): "One God, equality, honest living, service to humanity."
  ⚠️ "Militarization historically led to conflict."
  🎯 "Egalitarian society free of caste/ritual, union with God."
  ⚔️ "God (Waheguru) vs ego/haumai; battled through meditation and community."

- **Bahá'í Faith** (19th century Abrahamic offshoot): "Unity of God, religion, and humanity for world peace."
  ⚠️ "Seen as syncretic or overly idealistic."
  🎯 "Unified global civilization without prejudice."
  ⚔️ "God vs forces of division; overcome through consultation and oneness."

- **Stoicism** (Greco-Roman philosophy, influential in West): "Virtue through reason and acceptance of fate is the path to eudaimonia."
  ⚠️ "Suppresses natural emotions, promoting apathy."
  🎯 "Society of rational, resilient individuals."
  ⚔️ "Reason vs destructive passions; personal discipline, no supernatural forces."

- **Secular Humanism/Atheism** (Modern worldview): "Human reason, science, and ethics without supernatural belief."
  ⚠️ "Lacks transcendent moral foundation, risking relativism."
  🎯 "Progressive, equitable society advancing knowledge and well-being."
  ⚔️ "Humanity vs ignorance/superstition; battled via education, science, and law."
