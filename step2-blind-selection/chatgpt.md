## 10 most useful for humanity (from the attached taxonomy)

* **Secular Humanism** — centers dignity, rights, science, pluralism, and institutional problem-solving. 
* **Humanism (broad)** — strong civic-ethical focus without requiring coercive dogma. 
* **Liberalism (political)** — rights, constrained power, public justification of authority. 
* **Democracy (ideal-type)** — accountable governance, participation, and correction mechanisms. 
* **Buddhism (especially compassion-centered currents)** — reduces harm via anti-greed/anti-hatred ethics and disciplined mind training. 
* **Jainism** — radical nonviolence as a civilizational ethic (high protection against cruelty). 
* **Sikhism** — equality, service, honesty, and community responsibility. 
* **Bahá’í Faith** — explicit unity-of-humanity and anti-prejudice orientation. 
* **Confucianism (ethical-governance reading)** — merit, education, social duty, public virtue (when decoupled from rigid hierarchy). 
* **Pragmatism (truth tradition)** — fallibilism + experimentation, useful for peaceful institutional adaptation.
