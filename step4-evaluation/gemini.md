### **Audit Summary**

This report evaluates five belief systems using the **Weighted Harm-Adjusted Benefit Score (WHABS)**. Scores reflect verified outcomes, legal structures, and current human rights impacts, prioritizing lived reality over theological self-description.

## Judaism

### Question Scores (0–100)

**Q1 Apostasy (10%): 85**
No state death penalty or criminalization exists. However, Haredi/Ultra-Orthodox communities practice severe social shunning, "erasure" from families, and custody battles against leavers.

**Q2 Violence (15%): 80**
Historically powerless, preventing large-scale holy war. Modern defensive doctrines (pikuach nefesh) are strong, but religious-nationalist settler violence and the invocation of "Amalek" in current conflict degrade the score.

**Q3 Women (12%): 65**
Highly bifurcated. Liberal streams have full equality/female clergy. Orthodox/state-law contexts in Israel enforce inequality in divorce (agunah), and some sects practice erasure of women from public media.

**Q4 Children (9%): 75**
Strong tradition of literacy and questioning. Haredi sector penalized for censorship of secular subjects (math/science) and specific physical risks like *metzitzah b'peh*.

**Q5 Truth/Science (10%): 88**
Disproportionately high scientific achievement (22% of Nobel prizes) and acceptance of evolution in non-Haredi streams.

**Q6 Money (7%): 80**
High transparency in lay organizations and strong philanthropy. Some opacity and state-dependency in Haredi institutions.

**Q7 Outsiders (12%): 65**
Liberal streams are inclusive. Orthodox law maintains theological hierarchies; religious-nationalism in Israel is linked to discriminatory dual-legal systems in occupied territories.

**Q8 Founder (6%): 85**
Moses is depicted as flawed, reluctant, and dies without dynastic wealth; narrative allows for humanization.

**Q9 Governance (12%): 65**
Israel functions as a democracy with independent courts. However, the rabbinate's monopoly on personal status and the "apartheid" characterization of West Bank control by rights groups lowers the score.

**Q10 Psychology (4%): 75**
Low reliance on hell-fire/terror concepts. High control/surveillance limited to insular Haredi sects.

**Q11 Reform (3%): 90**
Strongest track record of internal theological adaptation (Reform/Conservative movements) without violence.

### Weighted subtotal

= 75.6

### Penalties

**Uncertainty penalty = 0** (High confidence; evidence consistent across sources).
**Red-line penalty = 0** (No state-level death penalty or systematic physical persecution of minorities).

### Final

**Final score = 75.6 / 100**
**Confidence = A**
**Tier = Mostly beneficial with notable harms**

## Christianity

### Question Scores (0–100)

**Q1 Apostasy (10%): 90**
Legal penalties abolished in almost all contexts; Vatican II explicitly affirms religious freedom. Harms are now limited to social shunning in fundamentalist sects.

**Q2 Violence (15%): 82**
Massive historical body count (Crusades, Colonialism). Present status is high due to widespread acceptance of pacifism/just war and peace-building efforts.

**Q3 Women (12%): 70**
Major split: Protestants ordain women and led suffrage movements; Catholic/Orthodox exclude women from authority and restrict reproductive rights.

**Q4 Children (9%): 65**
Built the university system. Score penalized heavily for the massive, global scale of clergy sex abuse scandals and cover-ups.

**Q5 Truth/Science (10%): 75**
Foundational role in science history, now largely accepting of evolution. Penalized for historical suppression (Galileo) and ongoing Evangelical creationism/climate denial.

**Q6 Money (7%): 70**
Largest global provider of non-gov healthcare. Penalized for Vatican financial opacity and predatory "Prosperity Gospel" exploitation.

**Q7 Outsiders (12%): 70**
Improving LGBTQ+ acceptance in West; severe anti-LGBTQ+ activity in Africa/conservative US (legislating death penalties in Uganda).

**Q8 Founder (6%): 85**
Jesus depicted as non-violent, poor, and executed by the state; provides no textual cover for war or wealth.

**Q9 Governance (12%): 85**
Best modern outcomes correlate with Christian-majority secular democracies. Theocratic history was oppressive, but current practice supports free institutions.

**Q10 Psychology (4%): 60**
"Hell house" tactics and Original Sin doctrine link to religious trauma and anxiety in children.

**Q11 Reform (3%): 85**
Proven capacity to change (slavery, science, women), though often through schism rather than internal consensus.

### Weighted subtotal

= 77.2

### Penalties

**Uncertainty penalty = 0**
**Red-line penalty = 0** (Active harms like LGBTQ laws in Uganda are severe but not systemic to the whole faith).

### Final

**Final score = 77.2 / 100**
**Confidence = A**
**Tier = Mostly beneficial with notable harms**

## Islam Sunni

### Question Scores (0–100)

**Q1 Apostasy (10%): 30**
Death penalty remains on the books in ~6 countries and is endorsed by all four classical schools of jurisprudence. Vigilante violence and family danger for leavers are high.

**Q2 Violence (15%): 40**
Historical rules of war were advanced for their time, but offensive jihad doctrines remain available and are actively used by terror groups.

**Q3 Women (12%): 35**
Structural legal inequality in inheritance (half share), testimony, and divorce is codified in most Sunni-majority family laws. High rates of child marriage in specific regions.

**Q4 Children (9%): 45**
Risk of radicalization in uncontrolled madrasas; emphasis on rote memorization over critical inquiry.

**Q5 Truth/Science (10%): 40**
Golden Age was brilliant, but modern "closing of the gate of ijtihad" and widespread rejection of evolution in curricula lower the score.

**Q6 Money (7%): 50**
Zakat is a strong redistribution mechanism, but lack of transparency allows diversion to terror financing.

**Q7 Outsiders (12%): 30**

**Q8 Founder (6%): 40**
Founder was a military statesman who ordered executions (Banu Qurayza) and contracted marriage with a child (Aisha), creating challenging legal precedents.

**Q9 Governance (12%): 35**
Strong correlation between Sharia-based governance and lack of freedom ("Not Free" status common).

**Q10 Psychology (4%): 40**
Vivid hell-fire pedagogy for children and social surveillance (honor culture) create high anxiety/guilt.

**Q11 Reform (3%): 30**
