## Q1: The Right to Leave (Apostasy)

### Judaism

**✓ Evidence of good**: The Talmudic legal process made capital punishment effectively impossible to implement (requiring stringent warning and witness criteria). Modern secular Jewish identity allows for leaving the faith without threat of physical violence.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Deuteronomy 13:6–10 mandates death for apostates. In ultra-Orthodox (Haredi) communities, those who leave ("OTD" - Off the Derech) face "Herem" (shunning), erasure from family photos, and community-funded legal battles to strip them of child custody.
**Current status**: No state execution; severe social and familial ostracism exists only in fundamentalist sects.

### Christianity

**✓ Evidence of good**: Modern Christianity, particularly in secular democracies, generally accepts the right to leave. The Vatican II declaration *Dignitatis Humanae* (1965) formally recognized religious freedom.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Historical record includes the Inquisition and execution of heretics (e.g., Giordano Bruno). Doctrinal threats of "eternal damnation" (Hell) act as psychological retention mechanisms. Fundamentalist groups practiced "disconnection" or shunning.
**Current status**: Legal penalties abolished; retention relies on psychological fear and social pressure rather than physical force.

### Islam Sunni

**✓ Evidence of good**: Some modern scholars (e.g., those associated with the Al-Azhar in specific contexts) argue that apostasy is a sin punishable by God, not a state crime, citing "There is no compulsion in religion" (Quran 2:256).
**✗ Evidence of harm**: All four major schools of jurisprudence (Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, Hanbali) traditionally agree on the death penalty for public apostasy. State enforcement exists (e.g., Mauritania, Saudi Arabia, Sudan historically). Vigilante violence against apostates is documented in Pakistan and Egypt.
**Current status**: Penalties range from execution and imprisonment in theo-political states to family disownment in social contexts.

### Islam Shia (Twelver incl. Velayat-e Faqih)

**✓ Evidence of good**: Distinction is sometimes made between "innate" (born Muslim) and "parental" apostates regarding the opportunity to repent, though this is a legal technicality rather than freedom of conscience.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Under Iranian Penal Code (based on Sharia), apostasy is punishable by death. Baha'is (seen as apostates from Islam) face systematic state persecution, denial of education, and imprisonment.
**Current status**: State-enforced capital offense in Iran; actively prosecuted.

### Salafism / Wahhabism

**✓ Evidence of good**: None documented regarding the *right* to leave; the system is predicated on purging impurity.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Strict enforcement of *Takfir* (excommunication leading to permissible killing). Promotes immediate execution of apostates without the lengthy tribunals required by classical schools. Documented summary executions by groups like ISIS and Al-Qaeda acting on Salafist-Jihadist theology.
**Current status**: Enforces the harshest penalties; zero tolerance for dissent, enforced via violence.

## Q2: The Body Count (Violence and War)

### Judaism

**✓ Evidence of good**: For 2,000 years (Diaspora), Judaism had no army and emphasized seeking peace (*Shalom*). Rabbinic law prohibits killing non-combatants in theory.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: The Book of Joshua mandates genocide against Canaanites. Contemporary: Religious Zionism (Hardal) uses biblical land claims to justify settlement violence in the West Bank. IDF operations in Gaza, while framed as defensive, involve high civilian casualties, with religious soldiers often citing biblical enemies (Amalek).
**Current status**: Historical powerlessness limited violence; modern state power struggles with religious justification for conflict.

### Christianity

**✓ Evidence of good**: Quakers and Mennonites have a 400-year record of absolute pacifism. Christian Truth & Reconciliation Commissions (e.g., South Africa) helped heal post-conflict societies.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: The Crusades, the Thirty Years' War, and the Taiping Rebellion (millions dead). Colonial genocides (Americas, Africa) were explicitly justified by Papal Bulls (*Inter Caetera*) and "civilizing" missions.
**Current status**: Mainstream theology largely rejects holy war; historical violence was catastrophic but is mostly retired in doctrine.

### Islam Sunni

**✓ Evidence of good**: The "Rules of War" established by the first Caliph Abu Bakr forbade killing women, children, and monks, and destroying trees.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Conquests of the 7th/8th centuries. The Armenian Genocide (1915) was framed as a jihad against Christians. Modern transnational terrorism (9/11, Al-Shabaab, Boko Haram) explicitly cites Sunni texts on Jihad to justify targeting civilians.
**Current status**: A struggle exists between mainstream "defensive jihad" interpretations and a violent minority utilizing foundational texts to globalize war.

### Islam Shia

**✓ Evidence of good**: Historical emphasis on being the "oppressed" rather than the oppressor. Fatwas exist against the use of nuclear weapons (Khamenei), though verification is debated.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Use of "human wave" attacks using children (Basij) during the Iran-Iraq war, promised entry to paradise. Creation and funding of proxy militias (Hezbollah, Houthis, PMF) that destabilize the region and target civilians in Syria and Yemen.
**Current status**: Violence is state-sponsored and exported via proxy militias under the guise of "resistance."

### Salafism / Wahhabism

**✓ Evidence of good**: None.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Theological justification for slaughtering Shia, Sufis, and "lax" Sunnis. Destruction of cultural heritage (Bamiyan Buddhas, Timbuktu shrines). ISIS implementation of sexual slavery and mass beheadings was based on Salafist literalism.
**Current status**: The primary theological engine for modern global Islamist terrorism.

## Q3: The Status of Women

### Judaism

**✓ Evidence of good**: Reform and Conservative movements ordain female rabbis and ensure full religious equality.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Orthodox *Agunah* laws leave women "chained" in dead marriages if the husband refuses a *Get* (religious divorce). In ultra-Orthodox communities, women are erased from media, required to shave heads (some sects), and segregated on public buses (illegally in Israel). Prayer at the Western Wall is restricted.
**Current status**: Full equality in liberal streams; second-class status in Orthodox streams.

### Christianity

**✓ Evidence of good**: Protestant denominations (e.g., Episcopal, Lutheran) have female bishops and leaders. Christian universities were early pioneers in co-ed education in the West.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Catholic and Orthodox churches forbid female clergy. "Complementarian" theology in Evangelicalism demands female submission to husbands. Historically, witch trials targeted women. Anti-abortion politics (Catholic/Evangelical) restrict bodily autonomy.
**Current status**: Deeply polarized; ranges from full feminist integration to patriarchal subjugation depending on denomination.

### Islam Sunni

**✓ Evidence of good**: Historically granted women inheritance and property rights (7th century) when Europe granted none.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Inheritance is legally half that of men. Testimony is often worth half. Men have unilateral divorce rights (*Talaq*); women must petition (*Khula*). Guardianship laws (*Wali*) in many nations restrict travel and marriage without male permission. FGM is prevalent in some Sunni-majority regions (linked to Shafi'i school in parts of Africa/SE Asia).
**Current status**: Legal inequality is codified in family law across most Sunni-majority nations.

### Islam Shia

**✓ Evidence of good**: High literacy rates for women in modern Iran (over 80%).
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Temporary marriage (*Mut'ah*) is permitted, which critics and human rights groups liken to religiously sanctioned prostitution. Mandatory Hijab in Iran is enforced by "Morality Police" with violence (e.g., Mahsa Amini death). Women banned from stadiums and singing solo.
**Current status**: State-enforced gender apartheid in public spaces and legal codes.

### Salafism / Wahhabism

**✓ Evidence of good**: None.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Total gender segregation. Niqab (face veil) often mandatory. Ban on women driving (Saudi Arabia until 2018). Women are viewed as a source of *Fitna* (temptation) that must be completely covered and confined to the home.
**Current status**: The most restrictive gender environment on the planet; women are legal minors for life.

## Q4: The Children (Indoctrination)

### Judaism

**✓ Evidence of good**: Emphasis on literacy and questioning/debating texts (Chevruta style) promotes cognitive development. Bar/Bat Mitzvah marks a transition to responsibility.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: *Metzitzah b'peh* (oral suction during circumcision) in ultra-Orthodox sects causes herpes transmission to infants. Haredi schools in NY and Israel often refuse to teach math/science, trapping children in poverty and dependency on the community.
**Current status**: Secular/Modern Orthodox prioritize open education; Haredi insularity limits children's future choices.

### Christianity

**✓ Evidence of good**: Extensive network of parochial schools that often outperform public schools in general education.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: "Hell houses" and scare tactics used in fundamentalist education. Documented massive scale of child sexual abuse and subsequent cover-ups by clergy (Catholic, Southern Baptist, etc.). Purity culture damages adolescent sexual development.
**Current status**: High variance; generally open education, but abuse scandals have shattered institutional trust.

### Islam Sunni

**✓ Evidence of good**: Emphasis on memorization of Quran builds discipline. Many Islamic schools teach full secular curricula.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Corporal punishment remains common in traditional Madrasas. Rote memorization often replaces critical thinking. In radicalized areas, textbooks teach hatred of "infidels" and antisemitism (documented in UNRWA and Saudi curricula reviews).
**Current status**: Education often blends piety with literacy, but critical thinking about religion itself is discouraged.

### Islam Shia

**✓ Evidence of good**: Strong emphasis on education and academic achievement in general sciences.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Indoctrination into the "culture of martyrdom" begins early. Children are taught to idealize self-sacrifice for the regime/religion. Public self-flagellation rituals sometimes include children.
**Current status**: State education systems are used as tools for ideological molding and loyalty to the Jurist.

### Salafism / Wahhabism

**✓ Evidence of good**: None.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Children are taught that music, art, and joyous interaction with non-relatives are sinful. Intense focus on fear of hellfire. Recruitment of children for combat roles in conflict zones (Syria, Somalia).
**Current status**: Systemic psychological abuse disguised as religious purity; restricts cognitive horizons.

## Q5: Truth and Knowledge (Science)

### Judaism

**✓ Evidence of good**: Jews have won ~22% of all Nobel Prizes, reflecting a culture that values inquiry. Maimonides (medieval) argued that if scripture contradicts reason, scripture must be interpreted metaphorically.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Haredi education often censors evolutionary biology and geology.
**Current status**: The belief system largely embraces science, with resistance limited to fundamentalist enclaves.

### Christianity

**✓ Evidence of good**: The Church founded the university system. Jesuits were major contributors to astronomy and physics.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Trial of Galileo. Opposition to Darwinian evolution (Creationism/Intelligent Design). Resistance to stem cell research. Historical burning of books.
**Current status**: Mainstream denominations accept science (including evolution); Evangelical/Fundamentalist branches actively fight scientific consensus.

### Islam Sunni

**✓ Evidence of good**: The Islamic Golden Age preserved and expanded Greek logic, medicine, and mathematics while Europe was stagnant.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: The "closing of the gate of Ijtihad" (independent reasoning) led to stagnation. Evolution is widely rejected in educational curricula across the Arab world. Pseudoscience regarding "Scientific Miracles in the Quran" is promoted as fact.
**Current status**: A conflict between a glorious scientific past and a present theological resistance to uncomfortable modern truths.

### Islam Shia

**✓ Evidence of good**: Iran accepts stem cell research and cloning (unlike many Catholic/Sunni authorities) due to flexible Fatwas.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: History and humanities are heavily censored to fit the revolutionary narrative. Dissenting academics are purged from universities.
**Current status**: Pragmatic acceptance of "hard" sciences for state power; suppression of "soft" sciences that threaten ideology.

### Salafism / Wahhabism

**✓ Evidence of good**: None.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Rejection of causality (occasionalism) in extreme theology. Historical skepticism of "western" science. Famous edicts (e.g., Bin Baz) previously questioned the rotation of the earth (though later retracted/clarified).
**Current status**: Deeply suspicious of any knowledge not derived from revelation; hostile to rationalism.

## Q6: The Money

### Judaism

**✓ Evidence of good**: Strong tradition of anonymous philanthropy. Social safety nets within communities are robust.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Ultra-Orthodox leadership in Israel controls substantial state subsidies while discouraging workforce participation. Financial scandals in some Yeshivas regarding misuse of government grants.
**Current status**: Generally transparent in lay organizations; opaque in Haredi institutional structures.

### Christianity

**✓ Evidence of good**: Largest private provider of healthcare and aid globally (Caritas, World Vision).
**✗ Evidence of harm**: "Prosperity Gospel" preachers exploit the poor for private jets/mansions. The Vatican Bank has a history of money laundering and opacity. Tax-exempt status is used to hoard real estate wealth without public benefit.
**Current status**: A mix of massive humanitarian utility and systemic financial exploitation by unaccountable hierarchies.

### Islam Sunni

**✓ Evidence of good**: *Zakat* (2.5% wealth tax) is a massive vehicle for wealth redistribution to the poor.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Lack of centralized auditing allows charitable funds (Zakat) to be diverted to terrorist financing (documented in multiple treasury reports). Gulf State religious endowments export ideology rather than pure aid.
**Current status**: Generous at the individual level; structurally vulnerable to misuse for political/militant ends.

### Islam Shia

**✓ Evidence of good**: Community support networks are strong in absence of state welfare (e.g., Hezbollah's social services wing).
**✗ Evidence of harm**: The *Khums* (20% tax) is paid directly to Marjas (religious leaders), creating massive, untaxed, unaudited financial empires (e.g., Setad in Iran) worth billions, independent of the state or people.
**Current status**: Financial structure empowers the clerical class with total autonomy and zero transparency.

### Salafism / Wahhabism

**✓ Evidence of good**: None.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Petrodollars have been used since the 1970s to replace indigenous, moderate forms of Islam globally with Salafist mosques/schools. Aid is often conditional on adopting strict religious practices.
**Current status**: Wealth is weaponized to homogenize global Islamic practice.

## Q7: The Outsiders

### Judaism

**✓ Evidence of good**: Does not seek converts; generally does not claim to be the exclusive path to salvation (Righteous Among the Nations).
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Halakha (Jewish Law) differentiates between Jews and Gentiles regarding saving lives on the Sabbath (historically). In Israel, non-Orthodox converts and interfaith couples face legal marriage restrictions. Hostility toward Christians/Muslims in radical settler movements.
**Current status**: Ethno-religious insularity creates a barrier to universalism; legally exclusionary in the Jewish State context.

### Christianity

**✓ Evidence of good**: Modern focus on ecumenism and interfaith dialogue. The concept of *Imago Dei* (Image of God) provides a theological basis for universal human rights.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: History of violent antisemitism (Luther, Pogroms). Colonization justified by "saving souls." Westboro Baptist Church style homophobia.
**Current status**: Officially tolerant in mainstream; historically responsible for systemic exclusion of non-Christians.

### Islam Sunni

**✓ Evidence of good**: Concept of *Dhimmi* historically protected "People of the Book," albeit as second-class citizens, preventing total annihilation.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Polytheists and atheists traditionally have no right to life. Yazidis were enslaved by ISIS based on Sunni jurisprudence regarding "pagans." Antisemitism is rampant in religious media.
**Current status**: Tolerance is often conditional on submission; minorities in Sunni-majority conflict zones face existential threats.

### Islam Shia

**✓ Evidence of good**: Historically tolerant of Christians/Jews as fellow monotheists in some eras.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Baha'is are termed "unclean" and have no legal rights in Iran. Sunni Muslims in Shia-militia controlled areas (Iraq/Syria) face extrajudicial killing and displacement.
**Current status**: Highly sectarian; identifies "enemies of the Ahl al-Bayt" for exclusion or persecution.

### Salafism / Wahhabism

**✓ Evidence of good**: None.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Extreme sectarianism. Shiites are considered "Rafida" (rejectors) and are targeted for death. Westerners are "Crusaders." Total intolerance for any difference.
**Current status**: Genocidal hostility toward anyone outside the narrowest definition of the faith.

## Q8: The Founder

### Judaism

**✓ Evidence of good**: Moses is depicted as reluctant, flawed (striking the rock), and dies outside the Promised Land. David is called out for adultery and murder by prophets.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Moses orders the killing of apostates (Golden Calf) and the slaughter of Midianites. The narratives normalize divinely ordained ethnic cleansing.
**Current status**: Founders are revered but humanized; their violence is contextualized or debated.

### Christianity

**✓ Evidence of good**: Jesus commanded "put away the sword." He held no political office, owned no property, and was executed by the state. His message focused on the marginalized.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Jesus introduced the concept of eternal torture (Hell) more explicitly than the Hebrew Bible.
**Current status**: The founder's life offers no justification for war or wealth, forcing violent followers to contradict his example.

### Islam Sunni

**✓ Evidence of good**: Muhammad established a constitution (Medina) inclusive of Jewish tribes initially. He lived simply despite holding power.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Led military campaigns. Ordered the execution of the Banu Qurayza tribe (700-900 men). Married Aisha (traditional sources say she was 6 or 9), establishing a precedent for child marriage laws. Accumulated wives and war spoils.
**Current status**: The founder's fusion of religion and military statecraft provides a permanent template for theocratic governance.

### Islam Shia

**✓ Evidence of good**: Ali and Hussein are revered as martyrs who stood against tyranny and corruption, emphasizing justice over power.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: The narrative of betrayal and vengeance fuels eternal sectarian conflict.
**Current status**: Founder narratives are used to mobilize current political grievances and justify "resistance" warfare.

### Salafism / Wahhabism

**✓ Evidence of good**: None.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Ibn Abd al-Wahhab (18th century) allied with the House of Saud to conquer the Arabian peninsula, explicitly using *Takfir* to justify slaughtering fellow Muslims who disagreed with him.
**Current status**: The movement was born in blood and political pacts; it remains true to that origin.

### Christianity

**✓ Evidence of good**: Separation of Church and State (Secularism) largely evolved within Christian societies.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Historical "Christendom" was monarchical and oppressive. Current attempts by "Christian Nationalists" in the US/Europe to erode democratic norms.

**✓ Evidence of good**: Malaysia and Indonesia offer models of Muslim-majority governance with relative development and democracy (though eroding).
**✗ Evidence of harm**: No Arab nation scores as a "Full Democracy." Implementation of Sharia (e.g., Brunei, Afghanistan) correlates with human rights abuses, censorship, and economic stagnation.

### Islam Shia

**✓ Evidence of good**: None in the modern context of governance.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: The Islamic Republic of Iran. Guardian Council vets all candidates, making elections a sham. Severe restriction of press, assembly, and personal freedom.
**Current status**: Theocratic Totalitarianism.

### Salafism / Wahhabism

**✓ Evidence of good**: None.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: The Taliban in Afghanistan. Saudi Arabia (historically). Total suppression of music, art, women's voices, and democracy. Public executions as spectacle.

**✓ Evidence of good**: Daily prayer provides structure and mindfulness. Community (Ummah) offers strong belonging.

**Current status**: High reliance on fear of divine punishment to enforce ritual observance.

### Islam Shia

**✓ Evidence of good**: Rituals provide emotional catharsis for collective grief.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Cultivation of a "persecution complex" and eternal mourning. Valorization of death/martyrdom overrides natural survival instincts.
**Current status**: Emotional manipulation focuses on grief and sacrifice to bind the believer to the leadership.

### Salafism / Wahhabism

**✓ Evidence of good**: None.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Extreme policing of thought. Every action is categorized as Halal or Haram. Joy is suspect. Creates a psychology of constant surveillance and judgment.
**Current status**: A mental prison of constant purity testing.

## Q11: The Capacity for Reform

### Judaism

**✓ Evidence of good**: Reform Judaism (19th Century) radically modernized liturgy and practice. Conservative Judaism adapts Halakha to modern times. Even Orthodoxy has "Modern Orthodox" streams.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: The Haredi world is moving rightward, rejecting modernity more aggressively. The Chief Rabbinate in Israel resists civil marriage.
**Current status**: Highly capable of reform in Diaspora; entrenching in Israel.

### Christianity

**✓ Evidence of good**: The Reformation. Vatican II. Acceptance of LGBTQ+ clergy in many Protestant denominations. Abolitionist movements were driven by Christian re-interpretation.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Schisms occur rather than unified reform. Fundamentalists view reform as heresy.
**Current status**: Structurally prone to splintering, which allows both progress and reactionism to thrive simultaneously.

### Islam Sunni

**✓ Evidence of good**: 19th-century Modernist movements (Abduh, Afghani) attempted to align Islam with reason.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: The "closing of the gates of Ijtihad" is a formidable theological barrier. Reformers (e.g., Mahmoud Taha) have been executed for apostasy. "Innovation" (*Bid'ah*) is a religious crime.
**Current status**: Reform is stalled; the most dynamic movements largely seek a return to the past (Salafism) rather than adaptation to the future.

### Islam Shia

**✓ Evidence of good**: The position of *Marja* allows for living interpretation, theoretically keeping law current.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: The concept of *Velayat-e Faqih* concentrates power, making political reform impossible without regime collapse.
**Current status**: Theological flexibility exists but is currently held hostage by political tyranny.

### Salafism / Wahhabism

**✓ Evidence of good**: None. The definition of the movement is to return to the practices of the first three generations.
**✗ Evidence of harm**: Explicitly rejects later scholarship or modernization.
**Current status**: Incapable of reform by definition; it can only be abandoned, not modernized.
